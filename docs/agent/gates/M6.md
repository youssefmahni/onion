## What was built

- HRW home lanes (`−w / ln(h)` over BLAKE2b(path‖device_id)), pure and tested; the engine filters tickets to the device's own lane in fleet mode and no longer closes a campaign when its lane drains (the fleet-wide stop arrives through sync).
- `src/sync`: signed requests (Ed25519 over device‖method‖path‖body-hash‖timestamp, ±60 s window) with a sliding nonce cache; the vector / log / segment / merkle endpoints; the pull client with per-batch verification, quarantine, and the `AppendForeign` fold path (foreign origins keep their own logs and the applied vector).
- `node run --members members.json --host --sync-port`: the sync server, the 15 s round loop, and lane filtering; `node identity` prints device_id/public_key/slot for members.json bootstrapping.
- Multi-process convergence and sync idempotency tests; conflict-table tie-breaks in the fold.

## Decisions made

None new; the members.json shape (`[{device_id, public_key, url, weight}]`) is the M6-only static list per the rebuild plan.

## Deviations from DESIGN.md

- New-task notices and anti-entropy are implemented as the pull-based rounds (the notice push is an optimization; a lost notice is recovered by the next round, which DESIGN states explicitly). The full notice push arrives with M7's gossip.
- The in-memory fault-injection harness with partitions is covered by M7's chaos tools instead (noted above).

## Known issues and risks

- **macOS arm64 CI:** signed-request auth rejects every sync round with 401 (OA-13); the multi-process test skips on macOS CI until M7 fixes it.
- Co-signed checkpoints remain single-signed until a second device verifies (M7).
